CH 05 · HOW TO

Set it up

Three screens on two devices. The assistant does the typing; you answer its questions.

{{ wizLabel }}

On the TV

  1. 1Settings → Device Preferences → About
  2. 2Press OK on Build seven times.
  3. 3Go back to Developer options.
  4. 4Turn on USB debugging, and Network or ADB debugging if your TV shows it.

Keep the TV on, on the same Wi-Fi as your computer.

On your computer

Paste the prompt into Claude Code, or another coding assistant with shell access. Put your TV model on the line that asks for it.

Open the repo

Follow along

Accept "Allow USB debugging?" on the TV when the assistant says it is coming. Then answer its checklist:

Which apps you use
Which four go in the dock
Keep the voice assistant, Chromecast, updates?

When it is done, switch USB debugging off again.
Nothing uninstalled. One line restores it.
